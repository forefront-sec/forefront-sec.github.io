Disclosure policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Scope

This policy covers forefrontsecurity.ai, our published research notes, our tooling, and the /.well-known/security.txt endpoint. It does not cover third-party services we use (Fastmail, Stripe, GitHub Pages, Google Search Console, OSV.dev) or client systems after handoff — report those to the operator.

How to report

Email security@forefrontsecurity.ai. Include the URL or asset, a clear description, reproduction steps, and the impact you observed. Please do not include secrets or personal data in the first email; we will ask if we need them.

What to expect

We aim to acknowledge within 3 business days. We will confirm the issue, agree a fix and disclosure timeline, and credit you if you want credit. If the issue is in a client system, we will forward it to the client and coordinate with them.

Safe harbor

For good-faith testing of our site and published tooling, we will not pursue legal action for accidental breakage. Please avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption. When in doubt, ask first.

No bug bounty

We credit; we do not pay a bounty (yet).
