How it works

1 · Scope & kickoff

Reach out via the founding rate above. We confirm the target list and contacts by email — no meeting required. Then we verify you control every target — a DNS TXT, well-known file, or response-header challenge we observe and capture as evidence, never a verbal OK.

2 · Sign & prepay

After scope is confirmed, we send the services agreement with the ROE as Exhibit A for e-signature. Company, primary domain, and primary contact are collected there. Full prepay follows countersignature; testing does not start before both.

3 · Gate clears

Testing starts only after the full gate clears: signed services agreement + ROE, payment, verified control of every target, and the ROE test window. Anything touching production needs your written OK — that's the line we never cross. Insurance details are available on request.

4 · Testing runs

AI-assisted testing run from our isolated lab against the targets your ROE names, every finding verified by hand before it goes near your report. You stay hands-off except for quick answers and production approvals. Methodology →

5 · Report + walkthrough

Typically within 10 business days of gate clearance: auditor-ready report written for SOC 2 evidence expectations — raw evidence, reproduction steps, indicative effort estimates, and a runnable validator per finding (exit 0 = fixed). The report walks through each finding in writing, worst first, so you can read it on your schedule and share it with your engineer or auditor. No calls are needed at any point; if you want to discuss the report once it's ready, that's optional and arranged after delivery. See what you get →

6 · Retests — 30 days, included

Fix a finding and request a retest within 30 days of delivery — we re-run its validator ourselves against your remediated system and append the result to your report's verification ledger within 3 business days. That re-run is the authoritative record; your own validator output is welcome but never the basis for attestation. One retest per fix attempt. The validators are yours to keep and run anytime, no engagement needed. After 30 days, further retests are a new engagement under a new ROE.
