The verification loop

1 · Hypotheses

Frontier-grade AI forms candidate findings — cheap, fast, high-recall.

2 · Cross-verification

Two models test blind and adversarially cross-examine each other's evidence before anything exists on paper.

3 · Human verification

A person confirms every finding. An unverified AI finding is a rumor with formatting — it never reaches you.

4 · Evidence ledger

Raw responses, byte-counted, UTC-stamped — disproven hypotheses included. Every finding traces.

5 · Validator

Each finding ships as a runnable script: exit 0 = fixed, 1 = present, 2 = rig error. You can verify the fix yourself; our re-run is the attestation.

Tooling, honestly

Commodity scanners driven by AI agents under human review — nuclei and ZAP anchor the standard stack, with Burp-class tooling where a web-app scope calls for it — plus custom verification tooling built in-house: rate-limited, non-destructive validators (bash + curl, exit 0 = fixed / 1 = present / 2 = rig error) generated per finding. The tooling is ordinary; the discipline is not.

Local-first, by construction

Frontier-grade AI on our own hardware.
The models in the verification loop run on our internal infrastructure — no third-party model APIs. Client code, credentials, findings, and evidence are processed on hardware we own; email delivery, payments, site hosting, search metrics, and public vulnerability lookups are the only third-party services. Nothing is sent to a third-party model API.

The hard lines

Never unauthorized. Ever.
No testing without a signed ROE · passive-only until it's signed · production touches need your written approval · no multi-step exploit chains in public content; every finding carries a single attack path in the report · findings go to maintainers and deployers first, coordinated disclosure always. And one more honest line: a completed engagement is not a security guarantee — coverage is documented in every report, and certainty is promised by no one.
