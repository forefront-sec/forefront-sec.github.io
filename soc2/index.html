What SOC 2 asks for

SOC 2 is an audit of controls against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria. Penetration testing isn't a single named control — it's evidence that supports several: how you detect and respond to vulnerabilities, monitor your environment, and manage risk. Auditors commonly ask for evidence that testing was performed (on which systems, and when), what was found and how it was addressed, and that the testing was done by a qualified party under an agreed scope.

Your auditor sets the specific requirement and the criteria they apply. This page is general information, not compliance advice, and does not guarantee any audit outcome.

FAQ

Will this pass my SOC 2 audit?

We don't guarantee audit outcomes — that's your auditor's call. What we provide is a report built so the evidence is verifiable, which is what auditors ask for.

Is a pentest required for SOC 2?

It's not a single named control, but it's commonly used as evidence supporting several criteria. Confirm the specific requirement with your auditor.

How fast can you start?

10 business days from gate clearance (signed agreement + payment + ownership verification + test window).

Do you need access to our code?

No — this is external, black-box testing of what's exposed. Staging credentials are optional and scoped in the ROE.

What if you find nothing?

You get the report and a scope & limitations statement showing what was tested. A clean result is still evidence — and it tells your auditor exactly what was covered.
