Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

1. Who we are

Forefront Security LLC, California, USA. Contact: hello@forefrontsecurity.ai (billing: billing@forefrontsecurity.ai; security issues: security@forefrontsecurity.ai).

2. What we collect via the Site

  • Email you send us (e.g., hello@, security@) and our replies.
  • If you request a free Public Info Scan: the domain name you claim and proof of ownership (a DNS TXT record, well-known file, or response-header challenge you publish). The scan itself uses only public sources (certificate logs, DNS/email configuration records, public indexing services, public code repositories) and does not probe your systems.
  • The Site runs no analytics, no cookies, and no visitor tracking. Search visibility is measured only via Search Console (aggregate queries and clicks from search results — no individual visitor data).

3. Engagement data

For paying engagements, data handling is governed by your Master Services Agreement and ROE. Engagement work runs on our isolated local infrastructure — no third-party model APIs. Client code, credentials, findings, and evidence are processed on hardware we own; email delivery, payments, site hosting, search metrics, and public vulnerability lookups are the only third-party services: Fastmail, Stripe, GitHub Pages, Google Search Console, and OSV.dev/GHSA.

Engagement records — the signed agreement/ROE, the delivered report, and its evidence — are retained for the period in your MSA (12 months by default). The retest window is 30 days after delivery.

4. What we never do

  • We never probe, scan, or test a system without a signed authorization for that system, or a verified ownership challenge for passive collection.
  • We never sell personal information. We have no ads and no data brokers.

5. Retention

Site correspondence is kept as long as needed for the business relationship. Raw scan-request data (claimed domain + requester email) is deleted after 30 days — kept only that long for abuse prevention. The email thread itself is correspondence and is kept as described above. Scan results are point-in-time; aggregate, anonymized metrics may be retained for published trust stats.

6. Access and deletion

Want your personal information deleted, or a copy of what we hold? Email hello@forefrontsecurity.ai. We'll honor requests except for records we must keep (signed authorization documents, engagement records, and anything required by law). We do not sell personal information — no ads, no data brokers, no exceptions.

7. Security

Mail is hosted by Fastmail (US/EU data centers); the Site is hosted on GitHub Pages as static files. No payment data touches our systems — payments run on Stripe.

8. Changes

The date above will change when this policy is updated.
